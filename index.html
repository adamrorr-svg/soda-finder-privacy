Soda Finder Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Soda Finder shows which soda brands nearby restaurants serve. It is made by Adam Orr ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short:

  • You don't create an account or give us your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your location is used to find nearby restaurants. We don't store it.
  • Soda reports you share are public, tied to a random anonymous ID, not to you.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your information.

Information the app uses

Your location

With your permission, the app reads your device's location to find restaurants near you. Your coordinates and chosen search distance are sent to our server, which asks Google Places for nearby restaurants and returns the results to your phone. We do not store your location. Google receives the search location to answer it; see Google's Privacy Policy. You can deny or turn off location access at any time in your device settings; the app then can't show nearby restaurants.

Anonymous ID

The first time the app connects, it creates an anonymous account for your device, identified by a random ID. It has no name, email or password attached. The ID lets you change your own reports, limits how many searches each device can make per day (we store the ID with a daily count), and lets us remove abusive content. The app shows this ID in its About section.

Spam protection (hCaptcha)

When the anonymous account is created, the app runs an hCaptcha check to stop bots. hCaptcha may collect device and browser information and your IP address for this purpose; see the hCaptcha Privacy Policy.

Reports you share

When you report what a restaurant serves, we store the restaurant's ID and name (or the chain's name, if you report for every location), the sodas you selected or typed, the time, and your anonymous ID. Reports are shown to everyone who uses Soda Finder. Please don't type personal information into soda names.

Problem reports and hiding people

If you use "Report a problem", we store your anonymous ID, the report you flagged, its author's anonymous ID and the reason, so we can review it. Flags are not visible to other users. "Hide this person's reports" is saved only on your device.

Information kept only on your device

Your chosen soda, search distance, preferred maps app, the people you've hidden, copies of your own reports, and whether you've accepted the community rules are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

Opening a maps app

When you tap a restaurant's address, the app opens Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze with that restaurant's name, address or location. What those apps do is covered by their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or rent your information.

How long we keep information

  • Location: not stored by us.
  • Daily search counts: kept with your anonymous ID until the account is deleted.
  • Reports and flags: kept until you or we delete them, or the anonymous account is deleted.

Your choices

  • Location: allow or deny it in your device settings.
  • Delete your data: email us at sodafinder.app@gmail.com with the anonymous ID shown in the app's About section. We will delete that anonymous account, its reports and flags within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling removes the information stored on your device. Reports you shared stay public, with no link to you beyond the anonymous ID, unless you ask us to delete them.

Children

Soda Finder is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules let each device change only its own reports, and flags are visible only to us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app.
