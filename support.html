Why doesn't the app show restaurants near me?

Soda Finder needs your location. Check that location access is allowed for Soda Finder in your phone's settings, then pull down on the Nearby list to try again. Try a bigger distance (3, 5 or 10 miles) if you're somewhere with few restaurants.

A restaurant shows the wrong soda

Open the restaurant and tap "Report something different" or "Update what they serve". The newest report is what everyone sees. Chain information comes from each chain's announced beverage partner, and some locations differ.

I saw something offensive

Open the restaurant and tap "Report a problem". We review reports within 24 hours. You can also tap "Hide this person's reports" to stop seeing anything from that person.

How do I delete my data?

Open the Chains tab, scroll to "Your data" at the bottom, and copy your anonymous ID. Email it to us and we'll delete your reports and anonymous account within 30 days. See the privacy policy for details.
